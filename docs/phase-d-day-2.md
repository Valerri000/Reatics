Signup has five paths: space seeker, broker, owner with listings, broker and owner, and coworking provider.

A broker, an owner with listings, and a broker and owner cannot finish signup without a DRE number. A space seeker is not asked. One account can hold both paid roles. That account type is broker and owner. The broker directory includes it only after an owner admin marks the DRE validated. A content manager cannot mark it.

Coworking signup requires the referral checkbox. The checkbox uses the scope sentence and is labeled as waiting for the final wording. The provider phone and email stay off public listings. Inquiries keep using 1-888-518-9168.

An owner admin can mark a DRE validated and a coworking account verified. An unverified coworking account can sign in and cannot appear as a public provider.
